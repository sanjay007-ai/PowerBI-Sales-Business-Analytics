# 📊 Power BI Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance,
revenue, customers, orders, profitability, targets, and business performance.

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
High-level view of sales, profit, orders, quantity, profit margin,
monthly performance, and state-level performance.

### 2. Revenue Analysis
Analysis of revenue by category, payment method, city, and key sales metrics.

### 3. Customer & Order Analysis
Customer and order analysis including total customers, total orders,
average order value, order status, monthly trends, and top customers.

### 4. Business Insights & Performance
Management-focused analysis using sales targets, achievement percentage,
variance, profit margin, contribution percentage, city performance,
and business insights.
